# IB Physics HL — Definitions

A TI-84 Plus CE reference: the IB definitions you need to know, in words, with no equations. All the text lives in `src/content.c`.

## Test unit: electrostatics and electric currents

The left column on the home screen covers the 2026 test, point by point from the study guide:

| Tile | Covers |
|---|---|
| Charge & Coulomb | charge, Coulomb's law, charging by friction, contact and induction |
| Field & potential | field strength, potential, potential energy, superposition, work done moving a charge |
| Plates & Millikan | uniform field between plates, edge effect, Millikan's oil-drop experiment |
| Current & Ohm | current, conductors and insulators, p.d., resistance, resistivity, Ohm's law, thermistors and LDRs, Joule heating, power |
| Circuits & meters | series and parallel, ideal meters, Kirchhoff's laws, potential dividers, sensor circuits and their effects |
| Cells & EMF | primary and secondary cells, EMF, internal resistance, terminal p.d., charging |

The teacher's exact wording is used for the definitions of electric field strength, electric potential, potential difference, Ohm's law and EMF.

**Sketches** describes every field-line, equipotential and V-I graph you have to draw. **EMF & r lab** covers the internal-resistance experiment and how to analyse its graph.

## Every other IB theme

The right column: A Motion & energy, B Matter & heat, C Wave behaviour, D Fields, E Atoms & nuclei, plus Tools & tips (uncertainties and how to word a definition). HL-only topics are marked (HL).

## Mix-ups

Pairs of terms that are easy to confuse, such as EMF vs terminal p.d., ohmic vs non-ohmic and electric vs gravitational fields, compared side by side.
